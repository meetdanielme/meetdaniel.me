[Daftcode](https://daftcode.pl) is a venture builder that develops and invests in early-stage tech startups across various industries.

* Developed and executed integrated marketing strategies for five B2C and B2B ventures simultaneously, covering social media, content, and paid advertising across Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and LinkedIn
* Managed end-to-end campaign execution: strategy, content creation, paid media optimisation, and performance reporting

## DaftMobile

[DaftMobile](https://daftmobile.com) is a mobile app development studio specializing in innovative consumer apps and digital solutions, with a focus on entertainment and productivity.

* Refreshed the communication strategy for the team, targeting other game developers and positioning the DaftMobile team as experts in mobile game development through showcasing their work and culture.
* [Launched a blog on Medium](https://blog.daftmobile.com) with posts focused on mobile development, design, productivity, psychology in gaming, coffee, and social media marketing. It was Daftcode’s first publication on Medium and I helped in transitioning the rest of the company’s blogs to the platform.
* Developed multiple series of content for social media and the blog
* Ran social media communication (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn)
* Ran performance campaigns to promote blog content
* Created a DaftMobile brand hero, *Randy*, based on team members’ personal characteristics and quirks.
* Created the “Mobile GameDev Poland” Facebook group, first one of this kind in the country. It grew to 300+ members in just three weeks.
* Refreshed communication strategy for studio’s most successful game, Finger Hero.

### Tools:

Facebook Ads, Twitter Ads, Instagram Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Hootsuite, Canva, Medium

## Indoorway

[Indoorway](https://www.indoorway.com/en) is a smart indoor positioning and analytics platform that helps businesses optimize spaces and workflows using real-time location data.

* Led a communication refresh for the launch of Indoorway Hive, ensuring better positioning of new products and services.
* Produced a product launch video for Indoorway Hive and collaborated with the PR team on blog promotion and media relations.
* Managed performance campaigns targeting developers and ran social media, including the CEO’s LinkedIn.
* Developed a channel-specific content strategy: Facebook for general updates, LinkedIn for business and recruitment, and Twitter for developers.

## RocketLuncher

RocketLuncher was a food delivery app designed for corporate office buildings in Warsaw, providing fast and convenient meal options for professionals.

* Built the communication strategy from scratch, focusing on supported office locations in Warsaw.
* Created content series highlighting app benefits and ran performance campaigns to drive installs.
* Organized offline promotional actions and developed creative giveaway materials to engage office workers.
* Managed content creation across all platforms to ensure a consistent brand presence.
