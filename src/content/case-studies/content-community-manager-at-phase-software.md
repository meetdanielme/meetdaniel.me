**Location**: Berlin, Germany
**Period**: May 2018 – February 2019
**Company size**: 20 people

---

[Phase](https://www.phase.com) is a design tool startup focused on creating a modern, code-powered alternative to traditional UI/UX design software.

* Developed and executed the product marketing strategy, managing social media and running growth experiments.
* Built and managed the **Phase Community**, growing the Slack group from 1,000 to 2,500+ designers and organizing design meetups in Berlin.
* Launched **Phase Magazine**, publishing 14 issues, growing traffic to 10,000 monthly views, and producing a companion podcast with designer interviews.
* Represented Phase at design events across Berlin and Poland, giving talks at meetups and building industry presence

### Tools:

Slack, WordPress, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Adobe Lightroom, Canva
