**Location**: Berlin, Germany
**Period**: March 2019 – December 2020
**Company size**: from 20 to 50 people

---

[ACELR8](https://www.acelr8.com) was a startup-focused recruitment company that helps tech companies scale by providing in-house recruitment solutions.

* Joined as the first marketing hire and built the marketing function from scratch, reporting directly to the co-founder
* Developed a B2B demand generation and brand positioning strategy that grew client engagement by 150%
* Grew LinkedIn followers 10× through data-informed content strategy; managed Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram
* Owned the full content pipeline: blog articles, podcast series (A-Players), video content, and client case studies
* Launched and hosted the Talking Talent community and event series, attracting over 1,500 HR and startup professionals
* Led the company’s visual re-branding following the Sprint framework and design thinking principles
* Managed the website on Contentful and blog on Ghost, later overseeing a full migration to Webflow

### Tools:

Contentful, Ghost, Webflow, Figma, LinkedIn, Canva, Adobe Lightroom, Eventbrite
