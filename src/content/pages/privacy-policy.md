This is the privacy policy for `meetdaniel.me`, my personal website.

This site is a mostly static Astro website hosted on Vercel. I do not run user accounts, comments, a newsletter, ecommerce, or a database on this website.

## Information you choose to send

If you contact me by email, I will receive the information you include in that email, such as your email address, name, and message. I use that information only to respond to you.

## Hosting and performance data

This website is hosted by Vercel. Vercel may process standard technical information needed to serve the site, such as IP address, browser details, request path, timestamps, and similar server logs.

The site also uses Vercel Speed Insights to understand basic performance and loading behaviour. This helps me keep the site fast and usable. I do not use this to identify individual visitors.

## Cookies and local storage

The site stores your light/dark theme preference in your browser's `localStorage` so your chosen theme persists across visits.

I do not currently use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or WordPress/plugin cookies on this Astro version of the site.

## External links

This website links to external services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Threads, X/Twitter, Medium, YouTube, and others. If you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply.
